# Brief 01: Elia Bay Suites (Thasos boutique hotel)

**Purpose:** show the "booking system" service to hotels and rental owners in Kavala, Thasos and Eastern Macedonia.
**Status:** functional build exists in `../thasos-hotel/`. This brief is for the designer who polishes the visuals.

## Audience
- Primary: families and couples from Greece and Northern Europe booking a summer stay.
- Secondary (the real audience of the demo): hotel owners judging whether this looks professional and easy for their guests.

## Tone and look
Calm, warm, Mediterranean. Sand and olive neutrals, sea blue as the action colour, terracotta for urgency (last room, errors). Serif headings, clean sans body. Generous space. No stock-photo clichés, no carousel.

## What to design (screens)
1. **Home:** hero, search bar (dates + guests), room highlights, why stay, location.
2. **Rooms / results:** cards with price for the chosen dates, scarcity label ("Last one available"), empty state when sold out.
3. **Booking:** stay summary + guest form, clear total, cancellation note.
4. **Confirmation:** reference code, email preview.
5. **Admin:** occupancy grid (28 days) and bookings table. Functional, quiet.
Both Greek and English. Mobile layout first.

## Must keep
- "Demo concept" banner on every page.
- The search bar above the fold on mobile.
- Total price visible before the guest commits.
- Visible form labels and inline errors.

## Needed from design
- Real photography direction (rooms, terrace, olive garden, bay) or an illustration system to replace the placeholders.
- Logo for "Elia Bay Suites" (fictional).
- Component styles for buttons, fields, cards, states (hover, focus, disabled, error).
- Figma or a clickable prototype of the 5 screens, desktop and mobile.

## Content facts (fictional, keep consistent)
8 rooms + 1 villa. Room types: Garden Double (4 units, from 85 €), Sea View Suite (2, 140 €), Family Apartment (2, 170 €), Pool Villa (1, 320 €). Peak season 15 Jul to 31 Aug, minimum 3 nights. Payment at the property, free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival.
