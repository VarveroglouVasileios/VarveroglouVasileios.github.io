# Brief 02 — Taverna Meltemi (Kavala seaside taverna)

**Purpose** Demonstrate a bilingual menu and online table reservations to independent restaurants and tavernas.
**Status** Functional demo in [TavernaProject](https://github.com/VarveroglouVasileios/TavernaProject).

## Audience

- Greek-speaking diners and visitors planning lunch or dinner in Kavala.
- Restaurant owners evaluating the booking experience and daily reservation management.

## Tone and look

A family taverna by the harbour. Deep teal, salt-white paper, sea-glass green and a warm gold for the booking action. The harbour SVG borrows the lines and hatching of a woodcut. Georgia headlines give the home page the feel of a printed menu, while a system sans keeps labels and prices easy to scan.

## What to design (screens)

1. **Home** with a harbour illustration, date and party widget, hours, fictional address and three signature dishes.
2. **Menu** with seven sections, 24 bilingual entries, three combinable dietary filters and an allergen note.
3. **Booking** with a date, party size, available half-hour starts and visible guest fields with inline errors.
4. **Confirmation** with a reservation reference and message preview.
5. **Admin** with daily reservations, a seven-day peak occupancy table, seating, cancellation and reset controls.

Both Greek and English. Start with the mobile layout and keep the search widget ahead of the illustration.

## Must keep

- A visible “Demo concept” banner, `noindex` and the designer credit.
- Clear date and time labels, keyboard focus and inline errors.
- Only show times that can accommodate the selected party.
- Keep allergens visible alongside the dietary filters.

## Needed from design

- Harbour and dish illustrations for the demo, with a direction for photography of the harbour, charcoal grill and shared plates in a real client build.
- A compact Meltemi wordmark and wave motif.
- Consistent controls for selected, sold-out, invalid, seated and cancelled states.
- The working demo as the clickable reference for desktop and mobile.

## Content facts (fictional, keep consistent)

Family taverna on the Kavala waterfront serving Greek home cooking, grilled meats, fish and local wine. Open daily 13:00–23:30. Four tables for two, six tables for four and four tables for six, totalling 14 tables and 56 seats. Reservations hold seats for 90 minutes and accept parties of one to ten, with starts from 13:00 to 22:30.
